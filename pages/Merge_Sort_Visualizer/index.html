Key mechanics
  • Left Residuals (13–15): Unmerged left elements (l ≤ k) shift to the back of A from right to left (k downto l).
  • Right Residuals: If right elements remain, they are already in position at the end.
  • Final Copy (16–17): Merged elements B[0..t-1] copy into A[i..i+t-1].
